About / Focus

I am interested in how to make robots move and interact with the physical world. My focus is on optimization-based and learning-based control for contact-rich manipulation and legged locomotion.

Research Interests

Robot Dynamics & Control Optimization-based Control Learning for Robot Control Contact-Rich Manipulation Underactuated & Legged Systems

Selected Projects

Capstone systems that integrate the full control stack end to end — two in manipulation, one in legged locomotion. Code is released as each matures.

Contact-Rich Manipulation — Peg-in-Hole

Robust insertion under contact uncertainty and pose error (thesis-oriented).

  • Pre-insertion trajectory optimization (OCP) with contact-aware MPC
  • Impedance-based recovery and contact-aware search at the low level
  • Evaluated on success rate, peak contact force, recovery, and sim-to-real

Whole-Cycle Manipulation — Pick-and-Place

Reliable grasping and placement under collision and force limits.

  • Grasp planning with collision-aware trajectory optimization
  • Operational-space / impedance execution with safety filtering
  • Evaluated on success rate, cycle time, and placement precision

Legged Locomotion — Terrain-Aware Walking

Contact-aware locomotion over irregular terrain, toward whole-body control.

  • Contact scheduling and foothold selection with MPC
  • Whole-body control and low-level torque / force execution
  • Evaluated on stability, disturbance recovery, and terrain adaptability
